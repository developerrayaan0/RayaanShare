How to Use

Share files securely with anyone, anywhere in the world using a direct peer-to-peer connection.

Step 1 — Host a Connection

  1. Open the website.
  2. Click Host.
  3. Your unique Peer ID will appear.
  4. Copy the ID using the Copy button.
  5. Send this ID to the person you want to share files with.

Step 2 — Connect

  1. The receiver opens the same website.
  2. Paste the Host's Peer ID.
  3. Click Connect.
  4. Wait until both devices show Connected.

Step 3 — Send Files

  1. Select the file you want to send.
  2. Click Send.
  3. The receiver will automatically start receiving the file.
  4. Once completed, the download starts automatically.

Important Notes

✅ Files are transferred directly between devices.

✅ Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

✅ Both users must keep the browser open until the transfer finishes.

✅ Larger files may take longer depending on your internet speed.

Privacy

This platform uses secure peer-to-peer technology. We do not store, upload, or keep copies of your files. Every transfer happens directly between the sender and receiver.
